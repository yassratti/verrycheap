The main idea is to create a marketplace for digital products.

There will be sellers and buyers.

Sellers will be able to sell digital products.

Buyers will be able to purchase those digital products.

The digital products will be delivered through the platform we are creating, therefore we will need to create a system where the seller receives the buyer’s order, and the seller can deliver the purchase to the buyer, and the purchase will be received through the buyer’s dashboard.

This will be done without directly connecting the buyer and the seller. The platform will simply act as a connector between both parties, but in a subtle way — without a direct chat or anything like that.

The flow will work like this:

=> Buyer makes a purchase => Seller receives the payment and the order => Seller delivers the product => The buyer will see it in their dashboard in the form of an email and password for registration.

Because they will be purchasing an account.
	1.	The buyer will be able to choose whether they want to buy an account, or provide their own account so the digital product can be activated on it.
	2.	The platform will act as the intermediary for the whole process, both payment and delivery.

If the buyer purchases and chooses that the seller should create an account for them
